# US Navy Decompression Tables — Summary & Emergency Procedures

## Key Definitions
- **Descent Time**: Total elapsed time from leaving surface to reaching bottom. Rounded up to next whole minute.
- **Bottom Time**: Total elapsed time from leaving surface to leaving bottom. Measured in minutes, rounded up.
- **Total Decompression Time**: Total elapsed time from leaving bottom to arriving on surface. Also called "total ascent time".
- **Total Time of Dive**: Total elapsed time from leaving surface to arriving back on surface.
- **Deepest Depth**: Deepest depth recorded on depth gauge during dive.
- **Maximum Depth**: Deepest depth after correction of depth gauge reading. For pneumofathometer: deepest reading +1 fsw (0-100 fsw) or +2 fsw (101-200 fsw). **This is the depth used to enter the tables.**
- **Stage Depth**: Pneumofathometer reading taken when divers are on stage just prior to leaving bottom. Used to compute distance and travel time to first stop.
- **Surface Interval**: In surface decompression context: total elapsed time from leaving 40 fsw water stop to arriving at 50 fsw in recompression chamber.
- **No-Decompression Limit**: Maximum time a diver can spend at a given depth and still ascend directly to surface without stops.

## Three Modes in Air Decompression Table 9-9

### Mode 1: In-Water Decompression on Air
- Top row labeled "Air" under each depth/bottom time entry
- Enter at depth exactly equal to or next deeper than diver's maximum depth
- Select schedule for bottom time exactly equal to or next longer than diver's actual bottom time
- Read across row labeled "Air" to obtain required decompression stop times
- Last decompression stop at 20 fsw
- Total ascent time given in next column
- Repetitive group designator upon surfacing given in last column

### Mode 2: In-Water Decompression on Air and Oxygen
- Bottom row labeled "Air/O2"
- Follow air schedule to 30 fsw (or 20 fsw if no 30 fsw stop), then shift from air to 100% oxygen
- Oxygen stop times shown in **bold print**
- Oxygen stop time begins when all divers confirmed on oxygen
- **Air breaks: 5 min every 30 min on oxygen** (not required if total oxygen stop time ≤ 35 min)
- All stops deeper than 30 fsw done on air
- Stops on oxygen commence at 20 or 30 fsw per Table 9-9, in bold type

### Mode 3: Surface Decompression on Oxygen
- Follow in-water air decompression schedule (top row) through end of 40 fsw water stop
- If no 40 fsw water stop: surface diver without taking any stops
- Start timing surface interval when diver leaves 40 fsw
- Required oxygen time in recompression chamber shown in next to last column
- Oxygen time divided into periods (30 min each), half-periods (15 min each)
- **First 15 min always at 50 fsw in chamber**
- Remainder of oxygen time taken at 40 fsw
- If schedule requires only one half of oxygen period: diver spends 15 min at 50 fsw, then surfaces at 30 fsw/min

## Surface Decompression on Oxygen — Step by Step

1. Complete any required decompression stops on air 40 fsw and deeper
2. Upon completion of 40 fsw stop: bring diver to surface at **40 fsw/min**
3. If 40 fsw water stop not required: bring diver from bottom to 40 fsw at 30 fsw/min, then from 40 fsw to surface at 40 fsw/min
4. Once on surface: tenders have approximately **3 and a half minutes** to remove breathing apparatus and diving dress and assist diver into recompression chamber
5. Place diver in recompression chamber
6. Compress diver **on air to 50 fsw at maximum compression rate of 100 fsw/min**
7. Surface interval = elapsed time from leaving 40 fsw water stop to arriving at 50 fsw in chamber
8. **Normal surface interval should not exceed 5 minutes**
9. Upon arrival at 50 fsw: place diver on 100% oxygen by mask
10. Diver breathes oxygen for number of 30-minute periods and 15-min half periods indicated in table
11. First period: 15 min on oxygen at 50 fsw followed by 15 min on oxygen at 40 fsw
12. Periods 2-4 spent at 40 fsw
13. If more than 4 periods: remaining periods spent at 30 fsw
14. Ascent from 50 to 40 fsw and from 40 to 30 fsw at 30 fsw/min
15. Ascent time from 50 to 40 fsw included in first oxygen period
16. Ascent from 40 to 30 fsw (if required) should take place during air break
17. **Interrupt oxygen breathing with 5-min air break after every 30 minutes on oxygen**
18. This air time is considered dead time — oxygen time begins when diver confirmed on oxygen at 50 fsw
19. When last oxygen breathing period completed: return diver to breathing chamber air
20. Ascend to surface at 30 fsw/min

## Ascent Rates
| Segment | Rate |
|---------|------|
| Bottom to first decompression stop | 30 fsw/min (20 sec per 10 fsw) |
| Between decompression stops | 30 fsw/min |
| From last decompression stop to surface | 30 fsw/min |
| 40 fsw water stop to surface (surface decompression) | 40 fsw/min |
| Maximum compression rate | 100 fsw/min |

**If rate of ascent > 40 fsw/min: STOP THE ASCENT, allow watches to catch up, then continue.**

## Bottom Time Limitations
- Maximum depth: **165 fsw / 50 msw**
- Surface orientated air diving total in-water time per 24 hours: **4 hours**
- Back-to-back diving requires minimum of two chambers available and sufficient personnel to operate both simultaneously
- All diving time/depth maximum limits per HSE DIV Information Sheet No 5 / IMCA D014

## Emergency Procedures — Summary

### Bottom Time Exceeds Table
- Read down to deeper depths in Air Decompression Table until depth found that has schedule equal to or longer than bottom time

### Surface Interval > 5 Min
| Surface Interval | Action |
|------------------|--------|
| > 5 min but ≤ 7 min | Increase time on oxygen at 50 fsw from 15 to 30 min (+1 half period). Ascend to 40 fsw during subsequent air break. |
| > 7 min | Continue compression to 60 fsw. Treat on Table 5 if original schedule required ≤ 2 oxygen periods. Treat on Table 6 if original schedule required ≥ 2.5 oxygen periods. |

### DCI During Surface Interval
| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Type I resolves during 15 min 50 fsw stop AND interval ≤ 5 min AND no neurological signs | Increase 50 fsw oxygen time from 15 to 30 min, continue normal decompression |
| Type I does NOT resolve OR interval > 5 min | Compress to 60 fsw. Table 5 if ≤ 2 oxygen periods originally. Table 6 if ≥ 2.5. |
| Type II symptoms | Compress to 60 fsw on oxygen. Treat on Table 6. |

### Loss of Oxygen Supply in Chamber
| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Temporary loss | Have diver breathe chamber air, return to oxygen when resolved. Any time on air = dead time. |
| Permanent loss (50/50 available) | Multiply remaining oxygen time by 2. Air breaks NOT required. |
| Permanent loss (air only) | Multiply remaining oxygen time by ratio of air stop time to oxygen stop time. Allocate 10%/20%/70% to 40/30/20 fsw stops. |

### CNS Oxygen Toxicity
| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| First sign of CNS oxygen toxicity | Remove from oxygen, breathe chamber air. Wait 15 min after symptoms subside, resume at point of interruption. |
| Symptoms develop again or convulsion | Remove mask. Decompress 10 fsw at 1 fsw/min after symptoms subside. Resume at shallower depth. |
| Another symptom after ascending 10 fsw | Complete decompression on chamber air. |

### Omitted Decompression
| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Uncontrolled ascent, within no-decompression limits | Observe on surface for 1 hour. No recompression unless symptoms develop. |
| Omitted stop at 30/20 fsw, < 1 min surface | Return to depth of stop, increase that stop time by 1 min. |
| Omitted stop at 30/20 fsw, 1-5 min surface, chamber available | Place in chamber, complete surface decompression. Minimum 1 half period at 50 fsw. |
| Omitted stop at 30/20 fsw, 5-7 min surface, chamber available | Place in chamber, complete surface decompression, +15 min at 50 fsw. |
| Omitted stop at 30/20 fsw, > 7 min surface, chamber available | Table 5 if ≤ 2 oxygen periods. Table 6 if ≥ 2.5. |
| Omitted stop at 30/20 fsw, > 1 min, NO chamber | Return to depth of omitted stop, multiply air/oxygen stops by 1.5. |
| Omitted stop deeper than 30 fsw, chamber available | **Treat with Treatment Table 6.** |

## Treatment Tables — When to Use

| Table | When to Use |
|-------|-------------|
| **Table 5** | Type I only, complete neuro exam normal, asymptomatic omitted decomp (< 30 min), asymptomatic divers exceeded surface interval limits. |
| **Table 6** | **MANDATORY** for: severe Type I pain, any neurological symptom, Type II symptoms, AGE at 60 fsw, blow-up with > 30 min decomp omitted. |
| **Table 6A** | Severe symptoms unchanged or worsening within first 20 min at 60 fsw. Compress to depth of relief (≤ 165 fsw). |
| **Table 4** | Deterioration at 60 f after prompt treatment. Needs deeper recompression. 120 min at 165 f. |
| **Table 7** | Non-responding severe AGE, life-threatening DCI. 48+ hours in chamber. Minimum 12 hours at 60 f. |
| **Table 8** | Deep blow-ups > 60 min omitted decomp. Recompression to 225 fsw max. |

**CRITICAL: DO NOT USE USN Treatment Table 4, 5, 7 or 8 UNLESS DIRECTED BY DIVING DOCTOR QUALIFIED IN HYPERBARIC MEDICINE.** (Except Table 5 has specific criteria above where it is used proactively by Diving Supervisor.)

## Repetitive Diving

### Repetitive Group Designator
- Assigned after EVERY dive, even no-decompression dives
- Obtained from No-Decompression Table (Table 9-7) or Air Decompression Table (Table 9-9)
- Represents amount of residual nitrogen in diver's tissues

### Residual Nitrogen Time (RNT) — Table 9-8
- Enter table with repetitive group designator on diagonal
- Read horizontally to locate surface interval
- Read vertically down to depth of repetitive dive
- Time at intersection = RNT in minutes
- Add RNT to actual bottom time = **Equivalent Single Dive Time (ESDT)**
- Enter Air Decompression Table at ESDT for that depth

### Surface Interval Rules
- Less than 10 min: add bottom times of both dives, enter table at deeper of two depths
- More than 12 hours: no residual nitrogen, use actual bottom time (single dive)
- Double asterisk (**) in Table 9-8: RNT undefined at that depth — diver remains in same group during dive at 10, 15, or 20 fsw

### Policy
- **Only carry out repetitive diving in emergency project requirement scenario**
- **Maximum of 2 days**
- First dive max to group "O"
- Diver must not dive again until **12-hour surface period** (Note: Chapter 6 says 18-hour hyperbaric exposure — check specific context)

## Fitness to Return to Diving After DCI

| Condition | Lay-off |
|-----------|---------|
| Type I full recovery | 24 hours |
| Type I recurrence/relapse requiring further recompression | 7 days |
| Type II full recovery + doctor approval | 7 days or longer |
| Type II residual manifestations (Hyperbaric Medical Specialist) | **Unfit for diving** |
| Pulmonary barotrauma / pneumothorax | 3 months + HRCT + specialist review |
