# Chapters 1-6: Introduction, Regulations, Roles, Planning, Manning, Procedures

## CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION & PURPOSE

**Purpose:** This manual compiles the necessary information and procedures to conduct safe air diving operations. It covers quality assurance, risk assessment, control systems, personnel policies, diving procedures, and system engineering.

**Standards Applied:**
- IMCA D014 — International Code of Practice for Offshore Diving (ICoP)
- IOGP (OGP) Diving Recommended Practice 411
- HSE Diving Information Sheets (DIS)
- IMCA DESIGN D023 — DESIGN for Surface Oriented (Air) Diving Systems
- IMCA DESIGN D040 — DESIGN for Mobile/Portable Surface Supplied Systems
- IMCA D064 — Cylinder and Valve Thread Compatibility
- US Navy Diving Manual Revision 7a (SS521-AG-PRO-010)
- ADCI International Consensus Standards
- DMAC Guidance Notes
- Current national legislation

**Scope:** Minimum standards applied worldwide. Where local regulations differ, the more stringent standard applies. Subcontractors must bridge their own manual to this one or adopt it.

## CHAPTER 3: ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

### Diving Contractor / Employer of Divers
- Ensure compliance with regulations and guidance
- Prepare and issue Diving Rules
- Ensure suitable arrangements for emergency response
- Appoint OCMs, Supervisors, LSTs in writing
- Prepare project-specific Diving Project Plan per IMCA
- Maintain Diving Operations Logbook
- Ensure plant/equipment register maintained
- Ensure suitable diving location with consent
- Ensure effective communications with emergency services
- All technical aspects per legislation, IMCA, client specs
- All personnel qualified per regulations/contracts
- All divers certified medically fit

### Client
- Duty of care and statutory responsibilities
- Ensure HIRA for all work scope
- Provide adequate emergency support facilities
- Notify diving contractor of hazards (surface/subsea) in writing
- Provide details of hazardous substances
- Control diving operation under PTW system where applicable
- Allow sufficient time for diving familiarisation

### Client's Representative
- On-site responsibility for safe/efficient work
- Must be competent via training/experience/qualification
- Ensure permits maintained, restrictions enforced
- Inform OCM of changes/conditions affecting dive team safety
- Participate in offshore risk assessments

### Offshore Installation Manager / Platform Manager (OIM)
- Overall charge of area where diving takes place
- Kept informed of diving activities
- **Authority to veto** start or order termination of diving operation through Diving Supervisor
- **DOES NOT** have authority to order start or continuation of diving operation

### Master of DSV
- Responsible for vessel safety and all persons onboard including diving team
- Should not become involved in detailed management
- If any doubt about safety: consult Diving Supervisor then order suspension
- **Authority to veto** start or order termination through Diving Supervisor
- **DOES NOT** have authority to order start/continuation contrary to Diving Supervisor judgment
- Must meet DPVOA guidelines for DP vessels

### Offshore Construction Manager (OCM)
- Responsible for all project activities including diving and life support
- Preferably time-served diver and qualified supervisor
- Primary responsibility: safe performance of all project personnel, equipment, activities
- Liaise with Vessel Master for marine interfaces
- Ensure risk assessments (JSA, Toolbox Talks) undertaken
- Overall responsibility for vessel mobilisation/demobilisation and operational activities
- Liaise with Client's Representative
- Maintain QA/QC per standards/specifications
- Ensure compliance with legislative directives and company manuals
- Ensure PM/certification programme for diving system documented
- Action corrective measures from audits/inspections
- Delegate authority does not relieve responsibility
- **Cannot order start of diving operation** contrary to Diving Supervisor unless holding valid LOA and formally relieves Supervisor by signing on in dive logbook
- **May order termination** on grounds of safety or operational constraints

**OCM Qualifications:** Valid offshore survival course, valid offshore medical certificate, 15 years diving/supervisory experience, IMCA Diving Supervisor Certificate, Intro DP course (not required for fixed structures or 4-point moored vessels), valid Letter of Appointment (LOA)

### Diving Superintendent / Shift Supervisor
- Assist OCM, liaise on all contract aspects
- Ensure QA/QC philosophy maintained
- Ensure compliance with legislative directives and company manuals
- Implement health and safety policy
- Convene and minute safety meetings (not more than 14-day intervals)
- Review audits/inspections on periodic basis
- Present work scope/procedures to diving personnel for discussion
- Supervise familiarisation programmes
- Check mobilisation list and all equipment certification
- Ensure safety meetings held at not more than 14-day intervals and after any incident
- Project completion: supervise demobilisation, return hired equipment, hold debrief, submit report
- **Cannot order start** contrary to Diving Supervisor unless holding valid LOA and formally relieves Supervisor
- **May order termination** on safety/operational grounds

**Superintendent Qualifications:** Valid offshore survival, valid offshore medical, extensive diving/supervisor experience, IMCA Air or Bell Diving Supervisor Certificate, Intro DP (if on DP vessels), leadership training (not mandatory), valid LOA

### Diving Supervisor — PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY FOR DIVING OPERATION
- Safe implementation and completion of work programme within legislation and company procedures
- Central person of diving operation — clear understanding of project, equipment, personnel deployment, factors affecting diver
- Carries full responsibility of diving operation
- In direct conflict between shift supervisors: OCM acts as arbitrator

**Diving Supervisor Tasks:**
- Assist in mobilisation, training, familiarisation
- Be familiar with work scope, specialised procedures, saturation emergency procedures requiring SS diving intervention
- Obtain signed letter of appointment
- Liaise with shift team and opposite number for smooth handover
- Refer questions outside area to Diving Superintendent/OCM/Shift Supervisor
- Conduct all diving operations safely per company manuals
- Report concerns immediately to Diving Superintendent/OCM
- Maintain full, proper, legible records during shift
- Ensure plant/equipment tested, maintained, repaired per statutory requirements
- Refrain from activities impairing efficiency (alcohol/drugs)
- Monitor personnel performance, encourage safe working
- Liaise with Client/Representative via OCM
- Know emergency procedures
- Fully brief diver(s) prior to each dive
- Liaise with subcontractors, vessel crew, project engineers
- Ensure plant/equipment meets regulatory requirements
- Ensure each diving team member has copy of relevant company diving rules
- Maintain diving operation logbook accurately, sign records daily
- Ensure dive communication recordings of acceptable quality, retained for at least **48 hours**
- Ensure divers appropriately qualified with valid medical certificates
- Confirm divers fit to dive before each dive
- Keep accurate personal logbook
- Fully brief relief before absence (end of shift/break), complete shift handover log
- Familiar with ROV/air diving support procedures
- Liaise with Deck/Rigging Foreman
- Ensure daily diver logbook entries correct and countersigned, score out blank spaces
- **Must NOT dive while acting as supervisor**
- Attend debrief/lessons learned sessions
- Responsible for health of divers — from DCI treatment to medical problems — liaise with Diving Superintendent/OCM
- Doctor acts in advisory role; Diving Supervisor responsible for compression aspects of treatment until formally handing over
- Seek specialist medical advice if diver needs attention

**Diving Supervisor Qualifications:** Valid offshore survival, valid offshore medical, extensive diving experience, IMCA Air/Bell Diving Supervisor Certificate, Intro DP (not mandatory), leadership training (not mandatory), valid LOA, IMCA Diving Supervisor CPD scheme (mandatory)

### Relief Diving Supervisor
- Bears full legal responsibilities of Diving Supervisor
- Must fulfil same training/qualification criteria
- Must be properly appointed in writing
- Responsible to the Diving Supervisor who signed on the panel

### Trainee Diving Supervisor / Lead Ldr
- Air or saturation diver who completed approved trainee supervisor course
- Gaining offshore supervisory experience before sitting exam and gaining formal appointment
- **May only supervise a dive in presence of properly appointed diving supervisor**
- **May NOT relieve a diving supervisor for any reason**
- Diving Supervisor remains in charge, signs dive log, cannot delegate formal responsibilities to trainee

### Safety Delegate / Coordinator
- Two members of project team, one per shift
- Not totally responsible for safety — pays more attention to safety than normal when actively involved
- Addresses safety/welfare concerns of shift personnel, represents to supervisor or OCM

**Tasks:**
- Liaise with vessel Safety Officer
- Assist OCM with emergency drills (report on them)
- Active member of Vessel Safety Committee
- Be aware of HAZIDS/safety review results
- Encourage safe working
- Liaise with subcontractors on safety
- Channel safety suggestions to Safety Committee
- Inspect working areas regularly
- Ensure fire watchers aware of emergency actions
- Encourage SOS cards and responsible for collation/reporting for their shift

### Project Engineer / Field Engineer
- Reports to OCM
- Engineering aspects of project, liaise with Project Manager and OCM
- Write project-specific diving/engineering procedures, perform calculations
- If no project engineer assigned: duties fall to OCM

**Pre-mobilisation:**
- Liaise with OCM
- Ensure certificates/sub-contractor safety docs distributed
- Check equipment (quantities, condition, certification)
- Attend audits
- Advise warehouse/equipment
- Advise vessel of mobilisation (loads, weights, dimensions, deck plan, ETA, hazardous materials, sub-contractor numbers, vessel positioning)
- Mark deck positions
- Assist deck foreman with mobilisation
- Deliver equipment manifests
- Ensure sea fastenings
- Check documentation
- Distribute

**Project Briefing:**
- Deliver briefing to construction team (dive team, bridge crew, deck crew, ROV team, third parties)
- Overview handout including drawings, field layout, major items, hazards, difficult areas, specialised equipment, approximate water depth

**Informs workforce of foreseeable hazards from HIRA/PSR:**
- Max umbilical lengths
- Vessel setup
- Diving inside anchor patterns
- Platform intakes/discharges
- Heavy lifts
- Possible currents (spring/neap)
- Expected water depth
- High pressure lines
- Chemical presence
- Invite feedback
- Incorporate safer/efficient operations via Management of Change

**Task Plans:**
- Develop overall work plan with OCM
- Create concise task plans for each activity
- Checked with Diving Supervisor/Diving Superintendent before distribution
- Key area: identify hazardous operations
- Task plan preparation ideally 3-4 activities ahead of current task

**Daily meetings:**
- Take minutes
- Follow recognised agenda
- OCM normally chairs

**Installation Support:**
- Available in dive control/on deck
- Available for Client liaison
- Record disputed items
- Document extra work requests
- Note equipment failures/sub-contractor hours
- Assess risks on changes (JSA)
- Implement MoC
- Compile engineers log of events every shift

**As-Built:** Complete as-built documents and sketches during work

**Debrief:** Attend debrief/lessons learned (OCM chairs, Project Engineer records)

**Demobilisation:**
- Ensure equipment containerised
- Return manifest
- Identify third-party hire for return
- Containerise as-built docs/videos

**Close Out:**
- Generate as-built drawings/reports offshore
- Assist with client data dossiers
- Follow through debrief actions
- Pass info to PM for sub-contract close out

### Diver
- Reports to Diving Supervisor, through him to Diving Superintendent and OCM
- Responsible for performance in safe/professional manner and well-being of colleagues

**Tasks:**
- Qualifications/logbook inspected by supervisor — **originals only, no photocopies**
- Valid diving offshore medical certificate, **no depth restrictions**, valid for duration of project
- Valid recognised first aid qualification
- Encouraged to have Diver Medic Certification (renew every 2 years)
- Current logbook maintained with statutory details
- Declare any barotrauma/injury in previous 12 months prior to mobilisation
- Familiarised with system/equipment: LARS, chambers, personal equipment, deck layout
- Perform duties safely, professionally, expediently
- Report defects/doubts about safety to Diving Supervisor immediately
- Personally check equipment
- Fully briefed prior to each dive, knows how to use tools
- Maintain acceptable physical/mental fitness
- Maintain logbook daily, ensure entries countersigned by Diving Supervisor ASAP
- Alert to environment, alert for potential/actual dangers
- Assist in maintenance/repair of support equipment
- Use working period to expand professional knowledge
- Participate in safety drills/meetings
- Take directions from Diving Supervisor
- Report any illness/medical condition preventing safe diving
- Advise Diving Supervisor of prescribed/proprietary drugs or medications
- Refrain from activities affecting efficiency (alcohol/drugs)
- If job requires work beyond experience/capabilities: advise Diving Supervisor

**Diver Qualifications:** Valid offshore survival, valid diving medical certificate, HSE Part 1 certificate (or recognised equivalent) for surface air diving, first aid/diver medic course (as required)

### Standby Diver
- Qualified diver whose role is to provide assistance to diver(s) in emergency
- Responsibilities identical to working diver
- **During diving operations: fully dressed (without helmet/mask on), prepared to enter water immediately**
- Must be free of residual nitrogen from previous dives (Repetitive Group Designator A on US Navy Tables)
- Carry out standby diver equipment pre-dive checks with Diving Supervisor
- Ready to enter water any time, familiar with emergency procedures, state of readiness
- Remain calm, communicate with Diving Supervisor at all times during emergency
- **Must have completed minimum of 20 commercial dives** — not newly trained diver for standby duties
- At all times aware of communications and activities between diver(s) and Diving Supervisor (remote speaker, TV monitor with diver hat camera)

### System Technician
- Either electrical or mechanical trade
- During diving operations: answerable to Diving Supervisor and through him to OCM
- Responsible for planned maintenance of all equipment under control, repairs, ordering spares, upkeep of records
- **Must not carry out maintenance or take off-line operational equipment related to work in hand without approval of on-shift Diving Supervisor**
- Maintain record of off-line equipment so shift change briefing complete
- Isolation labels on controls where appropriate

**Tasks:**
- Pre-mobilisation check of all relevant machinery
- Assist in mobilisation
- Attend specialist equipment courses
- Read marine ops manuals and QA/safety manuals related to diving plant
- Perform planned maintenance per PMS
- Undertake repairs
- Order spares
- Liaise with Diving Supervisor on equipment status
- Work per manufacturers and company instructions
- Maintain full records for each item
- Advise OCM of potential/actual defects
- Prepare hired equipment for return
- Notify Project Engineer when ready
- Ensure PMS records up to date

**Qualifications:** Valid offshore survival, valid offshore medical, valid LOA, electrical or mechanical trade qualification

### Foreman Rigger
- Responsible to Diving Supervisor/Superintendent/OCM for all rigging undertaken by company and action of riggers in charge
- **If Diving Supervisor requires unsafe work: direct matter to Shift Safety Delegate, Superintendent, or OCM**

**Tasks:**
- Supervise deck operations
- Maintain high safe rigging standards
- Ensure all lifting rigging (shackles, slings, strops) in good condition and certified before use
- Clear unambiguous directions to crane driver
- Ensure rigging crew briefed on work scope and safety precautions including Toolbox Talks
- Ensure rigging deployed to divers rigged safely and marked (e.g., light sticks)
- Ensure project rigging colour-coded per project requirements
- Return all project rigging on completion
- Tag out defective/suspected defective rigging with clear defect directions

### Rigger
- Directly responsible to Foreman Rigger, through him to Diving Supervisor/Superintendent/OCM
- Not undertake rigging not per safe working practices
- If doubt about safety: bring to attention of Rigger Foreman
- Use only rigging gear in good condition and in-date for certification
- Ensure rigging equipment deployed to divers rigged safely and marked
- Keep deck area clean and free of unused equipment

### Vessel Administrator / Technical Clerk
- Support role to project team, reports to OCM
- General administrative support — secretarial for OCM and Project Engineers
- **Support role only — responsibility for ensuring admin duties carried out per company requirements remains with Senior Offshore Representative**

**Tasks:**
- Organise crew changes with personnel dept
- Prepare draft daily report (submit to OCM, distribute)
- Record and distribute meeting minutes
- Maintain and submit timesheets
- Maintain files on familiarisations and emergency drills
- Assist in dive plan preparation
- Assist maintenance of project records (dive logs, gas reports, chamber sheets)

### Inspection Engineer
- Reports to OCM
- Control inspection content of work, ensure project objectives attained
- QC of all offshore inspection reports (manuscripts, computerised data records, video records, photographic records)
- Coordinate reporting data from saturation diving, surface supplied diving, and ROV survey
- Correct implementation of inspection techniques and proper use of personnel
- Detailed planning and preparation for inspection programmes, ensure departments/personnel briefed and aware of objectives
- Check all inspection equipment and consumables available, servicing and logistics of specialised inspection equipment under control

**Qualifications:** Valid offshore survival, valid offshore medical, engineering graduate or time-served technician with final C+G or HND/HNC or equivalent in relevant discipline, or CSWIP 3.4U
